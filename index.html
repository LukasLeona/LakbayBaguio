Before you ride

Small reminders for a smoother trip

01 🚌

Confirm before boarding

Ask the driver or dispatcher whether the jeepney passes your exact destination and where to get off.

02 ☁

Allow a weather buffer

Rain, fog, and weekend crowds can add waiting and travel time. Keep your schedule flexible.

03 💵

Carry small bills

Exact or smaller denominations make jeepney and taxi payments easier, especially during busy hours.

04 🚶

Use walkable clusters

Several city-center landmarks are close enough to visit on foot, avoiding unnecessary rides.
